AI PRODUCT MANAGER REPORT
==================================================

ORIGINAL FEEDBACK:
I run a small design agency with 3 people. Every week I lose track of 
what I promised clients. I use email, Notion, and WhatsApp but nothing 
talks to each other. By Thursday I'm scrambling to remember what was 
due. I've tried project management tools but they're too complex for 
just 3 people. I just want something that tells me every morning 
what I owe people today.

AGENT 1 — JTBD ANALYSIS
==================================================
Here's the analysis:

1. **User Problem**: Forgetting promises made to clients and struggling to keep track of tasks across multiple platforms.
2. **Job To Be Done**: Stay organized and on top of client commitments to ensure timely delivery.
3. **User Motivation**: Reduce stress and anxiety caused by last-minute scrambles to meet deadlines, and maintain a professional reputation.
4. **Pain Points**: Information fragmentation across email, Notion, and WhatsApp, and complexity of traditional project management tools.
5. **Product Opportunity**: Develop a simple, integrated task management system that consolidates information from various sources and provides daily reminders.
6. **Suggested Feature**: A daily digest feature that sends a morning summary of tasks and deadlines, aggregating data from email, Notion, and WhatsApp.
7. **Success Metrics**: Reduction in missed deadlines, increase in user satisfaction, and daily engagement with the digest feature.
